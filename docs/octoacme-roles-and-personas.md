# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality strategies, ensuring features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They collaborate with developers and product managers to validate solutions and identify risks early.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and approach for features and releases
- Create and maintain test plans, cases, and automation
- Coordinate manual QA and acceptance testing activities
- Identify and log defects, tracking resolution through closure
- Validate acceptance criteria before features move to Done
- Participate in release readiness assessments

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Enable confident, rapid releases through comprehensive testing
- Reduce manual testing burden through automation
- Improve product quality and user experience

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- Test case and acceptance criteria reviews
- Defect triage and status updates
- Release verification and sign-off

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test coverage, defect resolution, and automation strategy
- **With Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria and define quality benchmarks
- **With Project Managers**: Provide quality status and risk escalation for release readiness
- **With Technical Leads**: Align on testing approach for complex technical solutions

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, design guidance, and architecture oversight. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture for major features
- Review technical designs and propose improvements
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers and conduct technical reviews
- Coordinate integration and dependency management across teams
- Champion code quality, testing, and observability standards

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable rapid, safe feature delivery through solid architecture
- Foster a culture of technical excellence

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Cross-team technical coordination meetings
- Technical risk and dependency escalation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide technical guidance, mentor on best practices, and review designs
- **With Project Managers**: Identify technical risks and dependencies affecting timelines
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Align on testability and quality strategy for complex implementations
- **With Engineering Managers**: Support capacity planning and skills development

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, resource approval, and executive alignment. They ensure projects deliver business value and maintain organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics for initiatives
- Approve project charters and resource allocation
- Provide executive visibility and escalation path for risks
- Align projects with organizational strategy and priorities
- Remove organizational and business blockers
- Make trade-off decisions and prioritize competing initiatives

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain strategic alignment across initiatives
- Manage organizational dependencies and resource constraints
- Enable rapid decision-making and risk escalation

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval meetings
- Monthly or milestone-based business reviews
- Risk and escalation notifications
- Strategic alignment and prioritization discussions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Provide business priority, remove blockers, and review escalations
- **With Product Managers**: Align on business objectives and validate success metrics
- **With Developers & Technical Leads**: Make trade-off decisions on scope and resource allocation
- **With All Roles**: Provide executive visibility and strategic context

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design and UX Leads define user experience strategy, validate usability, and ensure features are intuitive and delightful. They advocate for user needs and drive design consistency.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define user requirements
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate designs through usability testing
- Ensure design consistency and brand alignment
- Collaborate with developers on implementation
- Advocate for user needs in prioritization and trade-off discussions

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, delightful user experiences
- Reduce user confusion and support burden
- Maximize feature adoption and user satisfaction
- Maintain consistent, accessible design standards

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and user research findings
- Specification handoffs and implementation feedback
- Usability testing and iteration planning
- Design system and consistency discussions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide design specifications and support implementation feedback
- **With Product Managers**: Validate user needs and advocate for usability in prioritization
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Define usability acceptance criteria and support user testing
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate design timeline and dependencies

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead development teams, manage capacity, and support career development. They ensure the team has the resources, clarity, and support needed to deliver.

### Responsibilities
- Lead and mentor the engineering team
- Plan team capacity and allocate work fairly
- Support professional development and career growth
- Identify team skills gaps and plan hiring or training
- Participate in project planning and risk assessment
- Remove impediments and support team well-being

### Goals
- Build a high-performing, motivated team
- Ensure sustainable, predictable delivery
- Reduce turnover and increase team satisfaction
- Develop technical talent and future leaders

### Typical Communication
- One-on-ones with team members
- Project planning and capacity discussions
- Performance feedback and career development conversations
- Team retrospectives and process improvements

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers & Technical Leads**: Support team growth, facilitate collaboration, and remove blockers
- **With Project Managers**: Provide capacity planning and escalate team-level risks
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborate on priority planning and resource allocation
- **With Stakeholders**: Represent team needs and negotiate commitments
